The note pages return HTTP 200, but their content is fetched from `/v1/notes/<slug>`. `robots.txt` blocked `/v1/`, and the app displayed “NOT FOUND” for any fetch failure. That explains why Google’s rendered page was classified as a Soft 404 even though the note opens in a browser.

I allowed the public note API routes in [robots.txt](/tmp/h-uIX2HK/site/robots.txt), changed [app.js](/tmp/h-uIX2HK/site/app.js) to show “NOT FOUND” only for an actual 404, and documented the cause and fix in [ANSWER.md](/tmp/h-uIX2HK/ANSWER.md). `git diff --check` and `node --check site/app.js` passed. No external service was changed.
