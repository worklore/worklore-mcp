# 1-Page Marketing Plan for Taller Lento

This plan focuses on filling your pottery classes. As you noted, classes provide stable, predictable income and are less weather-dependent than selling mugs at the fair.

## Phase 1: The Prospect (Before)
*Getting people to know you exist and what you offer.*

**1. My Target Market**
*   **Primary (Classes):** Women (28–45) in Chacarita, Colegiales, Villa Crespo, and Palermo working in office/tech/design jobs who want an off-screen, tactile escape.
*   **Secondary (Classes):** English-speaking expats and digital nomads living in Buenos Aires (since you can teach in English).
*   **Tertiary (Products):** Tourists and locals at the Palermo fair buying unique, handmade gifts.

**2. My Message to My Target Market**
*   **The Hook:** "Slow down and create something with your hands."
*   **The Pitch:** A calm, welcoming escape from screens in a cozy Chacarita studio. 
*   **The Differentiator:** Small, intimate groups (max 6) with bilingual (Spanish/English) instruction available.

**3. The Media I Will Use**
*   **Instagram (@taller.lento):** Focus on what works—Reels of wheel-throwing, plus Stories of the studio vibe. 
*   **Palermo Craft Fair:** Use your stall as a billboard for classes, not just a place to sell mugs.
*   **Google Maps:** Set up a free "Google Business Profile" for Taller Lento. It helps locals and expats find you when they search for "pottery class Buenos Aires" or "clases de cerámica Chacarita".

## Phase 2: The Lead (During)
*Getting interested people to give you their contact info and buy their first class.*

**4. My Lead Capture System**
*   **At the Fair:** Stop saying "follow me on IG". Put out a clipboard (or a QR code) that says, *"Want to take a pottery class? Leave your WhatsApp number and I'll text you when spots open up!"*
*   **On Instagram:** Change your Linktree text to say *"Message me on WhatsApp to book a Taster Class"* so the action is clear.

**5. My Lead Nurturing System**
*   **WhatsApp Broadcast List:** Save the numbers you get at the fair into a "Waitlist" contact group.
*   **Monthly Updates:** Once a month, send a WhatsApp message to this list: *"Hi! I'm running a Taster Class on [Date], and I have 2 spots open in my new Wednesday evening monthly group. Let me know if you want one!"*

**6. My Sales Conversion Strategy**
*   **Follow up after Taster Classes:** Tasters are your best leads, but you noted they rarely convert because you don't follow up. Two days after a Taster class, send a WhatsApp: *"Loved having you! The next monthly group starts on the 1st. Want to grab a spot?"*
*   **Incentivize the Jump:** Offer a small bonus if they sign up for a monthly class at the end of their Taster class (e.g., "Sign up today for next month, and you can pick any mug from the shelf for free").

## Phase 3: The Customer (After)
*Getting them to stay longer, spend more, and bring friends.*

**7. How I Deliver a World-Class Experience**
*   Keep groups strictly small (max 6) so everyone gets 1-on-1 time.
*   Provide high-quality materials and your signature glazes (like the yerba green).
*   Maintain a calm, relaxing, judgment-free environment where people can decompress from their work week.

**8. How I Increase Customer Lifetime Value**
*   **Fix the Month-1 Drop-off:** Students often leave because they lack a goal. At the end of Month 1, give them a "Month 2 Challenge" (e.g., "Next month, we are going to learn how to make teapots or matching bowl sets"). 
*   **Pre-book the next month:** In week 3 of the month, personally ask each student: *"Are you continuing next month? I need to finalize the list."* This secures the commitment before their "schedule changes".

**9. How I Orchestrate Referrals**
*   **Bring-a-Friend Perk:** Since friends buy mugs and join classes, tell your 3+ month students: *"If you bring a friend to a Taster class, I'll give you a free bag of clay for the month."*
*   **Instagram Tags:** Set up a clean, well-lit corner in your studio. When a piece comes out of the kiln, encourage the student to take a picture of it there and tag @taller.lento.

### Your 6-Month Action Checklist (Under 5 hrs/week & Under US$50/mo)
*   **Week 1:** Set up your free Google Maps listing. Create the "WhatsApp Waitlist" clipboard for Sunday's fair. 
*   **Week 2:** Message past Taster students. Tell them you are opening a new **Wednesday 19:00–22:00** group and ask if they want the first spots.
*   **Week 3:** Implement the "Month 2 Goal" chat with your current Saturday students to ensure they stay.
*   **Ongoing (Budget):** Spend your US$50/month budget on a simple Instagram Boost of your best-performing wheel-throwing Reel, targeting women 25-45 within a 3km radius of Chacarita.
