# 1-Page Marketing Plan — Taller Lento

**The offer:** Small-group wheel-throwing classes in Chacarita. **The sale:** A paid monthly class booking; a taster counts as a step toward it. **Six-month goal:** Saturday 6/6, Wednesday at least 4/6, and more students staying into month three.

| BEFORE — help the right people find us |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Who:** Women 28–45 in Chacarita, Colegiales, Villa Crespo and Palermo who want a calm, off-screen activity; include English-speaking locals and expats. Fair mug buyers are a source of class introductions. Goal: Saturday 6/6 and Wednesday at least 4/6 in six months. | **2. What to say:** “Tres horas de torno, sin pantallas, en un grupo de hasta seis, a 10 minutos de Lacroze.” Clay and firing included. Monthly class: ARS 120,000; “Probá el torno” taster: ARS 40,000. Offer the same details in English when asked. | **3. Where to show up:** Post two short wheel videos a week on Instagram, with location and a class invitation. At the Sunday fair, display a “¿Querés probar el torno?” QR code. Ask students to mention classes to friends. Add a Google Maps listing. Allow about 5 marketing hours a week; spend up to ARS 70,000/month on the sign and a small local Instagram test once booking works. |

| DURING — turn interest into a booking |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| **4. Get a contact:** Point the Instagram bio and fair QR to WhatsApp with a ready-made “Quiero info de clases” message. Ask whether they prefer Saturday monthly, Wednesday evening, or a taster; save their name, preference and source in a simple Sheet, with permission to follow up. | **5. Help them decide:** Reply within a day using short Spanish or English saved messages: dates, price, what is included, how beginners start, and a photo of students’ work. If they asked for updates, send one useful follow-up after two days and one when a relevant seat opens. | **6. Book the seat:** Offer a specific date and payment instructions in the same chat; mark the seat confirmed when paid. After a taster, invite them to the next monthly group within 48 hours. Fill Saturday first; open Wednesday when four people have paid for its first month. |

| AFTER — make staying and sharing easy |  |  |
|---|---|---|
| **7. Give a good first month:** Send a welcome note with directions, timing, what to wear and what they will make. Check in after the second class, especially if a schedule conflict appears. Make the next month’s dates clear before the current month ends. | **8. Invite them back:** In week three, ask each student directly whether they want next month’s seat; offer the Wednesday group when it exists and suits their schedule. Keep mug gifts visible, but protect kiln space for classes instead of taking low-margin wholesale work. | **9. Encourage sharing:** At the end of the month, ask happy students to bring a friend to a taster, share a photo of their piece, or leave a Google review. Track each week: class questions by source, paid monthly bookings, taster-to-monthly moves, and students reaching month three. |
