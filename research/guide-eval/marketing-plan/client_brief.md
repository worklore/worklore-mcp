# About my business — answers to the questions you would ask me

Don't wait for me, I'm not available this week. Everything I can tell you is
below; where something is missing, make your best assumption and say so.

## Who I am
I'm Lucía Ferreyra, a potter. My studio is **Taller Lento**, a one-person
ceramics studio in my converted garage in Chacarita, Buenos Aires
(10 minutes' walk from the Federico Lacroze station). I've been doing it
full-time for two years.

## What I sell
1. **Handmade mugs** (stoneware, my own glazes — the speckled "yerba" green is
   the bestseller). ARS 28,000 each (about US$20). Also small bowls at
   ARS 22,000 and the occasional set. My kiln and my hands cap me at about
   60 mugs a month.
2. **Monthly wheel-throwing classes.** One group: Saturdays 10:00–13:00, four
   Saturdays a month, max 6 students (I have 6 wheels). ARS 120,000 per person
   per month (about US$85), clay and firing included. Students keep what they make.
3. **Taster class** ("Probá el torno"): one Saturday afternoon, 2.5 hours,
   ARS 40,000 (about US$28). I run it when I have enough people asking —
   maybe once a month.

## Where the money really comes from
The mugs are what people see, but the classes are what keep the studio alive.
A full class group pays more than a whole month of mugs, it's predictable,
and it doesn't depend on the weather at the fair. Right now the Saturday group
usually has **3 or 4 of 6 seats filled**. About half of new students stay for
a second month; the ones who stay 3+ months are my best people — they bring
friends and buy mugs as gifts. I'd love to open a **second group on Wednesday
evenings (19:00–22:00)** if I could fill it.

When I picture "it worked", it's a stranger booking a class — ideally the
monthly one, or a taster that turns into the monthly — and then staying.
A tourist buying one mug is nice but it doesn't change my month.

## How people find me today
- **Weekend design fair** — Sundays at a craft fair in Palermo (stall costs
  ARS 35,000 per Sunday). Most mug sales happen here. Maybe 40% of fair buyers
  are tourists (lots of English speakers, some Brazilians). People pick up the
  mugs, ask "do you give classes?", and I say "follow me on Instagram" — and
  that's mostly where it ends.
- **Instagram** @taller.lento — 1,900 followers. I post when I remember,
  maybe once a week: finished pieces, sometimes a Reel of the wheel (those get
  5–10x more views). Captions in Spanish only.
- **WhatsApp** — all bookings happen by WhatsApp message. Payment by bank
  transfer or Mercado Pago link. I keep the student list in my phone contacts.
- Link in bio goes to a Linktree with my WhatsApp and a PDF price list.
- No website, no email list, no Google Maps listing.
- Word of mouth: most current students came from a friend of a student.

## Who buys
- **Mugs:** tourists at the fair (souvenir, "made in Buenos Aires"), and
  porteñas 25–45 buying gifts.
- **Classes:** mostly women 28–45 who work in offices or design/tech jobs,
  living in Chacarita, Colegiales, Villa Crespo, Palermo. They want something
  off-screen and calm, with their hands. Some come in pairs with a friend.
  A few expats/digital nomads (English speakers) asked about classes in English
  — I can teach in English fine.

## Problems I know about
- Students drop off after the first month, often with "my schedule changed".
- Taster-class people rarely convert to the monthly class — I don't follow up.
- Fair people ask about classes but I never get their contact.
- I can't ship internationally (too expensive, breakage), so tourists only buy
  what they can carry.
- Sometimes a café asks about wholesale mugs; I don't really want that — the
  margins are bad and it eats kiln space.

## My limits
- **Marketing time: about 5 hours a week, total.** The rest is throwing,
  glazing, firing, teaching and the fair.
- **Marketing budget: about US$50 a month** (around ARS 70,000), not counting
  the fair stall.
- I'm not a marketer. Please write it so I understand it without a
  dictionary. I'm comfortable with Instagram, WhatsApp and Google Sheets;
  I don't want to learn complicated tools.
- My audience is Spanish-speaking locals and English-speaking tourists/expats.

## What I want in 6 months
Saturday group full (6/6) every month, a Wednesday group running with at
least 4 students, and more students staying past their second month.
