## Foundation: The Item & The Sale

> *"Define the sale before anything else. Guess that wrong and half the plan rots."*

| Assumption | Definition | Strategic Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Item** | **Intimate Wheel-Throwing Classes** (4-week monthly cohorts & Saturday taster workshops) in a converted 6-wheel garage studio in Chacarita, anchored by handmade stoneware mugs. | Mugs build brand awareness and cash flow at fairs, but are physically capped (~60/month). Classes are the high-margin, weather-independent recurring engine that sustains the studio. |
| **The Sale** | **A paid monthly class enrollment (ARS 120,000/mo)** or a taster class (ARS 40,000) that converts to monthly; secondary conversion is **retention past month 2** across two cohorts (Saturday morning and Wednesday evening). | A tourist buying one mug is a single transaction; a recurring student generates ARS 360,000+ over 3 months, buys gift pottery, and refers friends. |

### The Unit Economics & 6-Month Target

* **Current Baseline:** Saturday group has 3–4 of 6 seats filled (~ARS 360k–480k/mo). 50% drop-off after Month 1. Fair stall generates mug sales but loses class leads.
* **Target in 6 Months:**
  * **Saturday Morning Cohort (10:00–13:00):** 6/6 seats filled consistently = **ARS 720,000/mo**.
  * **Wednesday Evening Cohort (19:00–22:00):** 4–6 seats filled = **ARS 480,000 – ARS 720,000/mo**.
  * **Total Class Recurring Revenue:** **ARS 1,200,000 – ARS 1,440,000/mo** (US$850–$1,020/mo), stabilized against fair weather and kiln bottlenecks.

## Square-by-Square Deep Dive

### Phase 1: BEFORE (Prospect) — Getting Them to Notice You

#### Square 1: Target Market
* **In plain English:** *Who, exactly, this is for — and whose problem you solve.*
* **Primary Segment:** Professional women aged 28–45 living in Chacarita, Colegiales, Villa Crespo, and Palermo working in high-screen, high-stress desk jobs (tech, UX, corporate, agency). They suffer from screen fatigue and burnout; they crave a grounding, tactile, meditative ritual where their phone is put away and their hands are in clay.
* **Secondary Segment:** English-speaking expats and digital nomads living in Buenos Aires who want authentic, small-group creative experiences taught in English (Lucía is fluent; very few BA studios offer intimate English-friendly wheel instruction).
* **Tertiary Segment (Top of Funnel):** Design fair visitors in Palermo looking for meaningful local artisan gifts (mugs/bowls) who are naturally pottery-curious.

#### Square 2: Message
* **In plain English:** *What you say to hook them and make them choose you over anyone else.*
* **Core Hook (Spanish):** *"Tres horas sin pantallas. Barro en las manos, calma en la cabeza."*  
  *(English: "Three hours offline. Clay in your hands, calm in your head.")*
* **The Differentiator:** Taller Lento is not an anonymous, overcrowded commercial pottery academy with 20 students fighting over wheels. It is an intimate, light-filled garage studio in Chacarita with **only 6 wheels** — one wheel dedicated to you for the entire 3 hours, personal coaching from Lucía, high-fire stoneware, and pieces you will genuinely love using every morning.

#### Square 3: Media
* **In plain English:** *Where you reach them without wasting time or money.*
* **1. The Palermo Sunday Fair (Offline Conversion Engine):** Lucía already pays ARS 35,000/week for the stall. Instead of letting class inquiries fade into "follow me on Instagram", the booth becomes a direct lead generator.
* **2. Instagram Reels (@taller.lento):** Process and ASMR videos (centering clay, trimming foot rings, glaze reveals) already yield 5–10× views. Post 1–2 Reels per week with bilingual hooks (Spanish first, English subtitle).
* **3. Google Business Profile / Google Maps (Zero-Cost Setup):** Currently missing! Creating "Taller Lento — Clases de Cerámica y Torno en Chacarita" takes 30 minutes, captures high-intent organic search queries ("clases de torno buenos aires", "pottery workshop chacarita"), and gathers student reviews.
* **4. Hyper-Local Boosted Reels ($40/month):** Use Meta Ads to boost top-performing Reels to women aged 26–45 within a 3-kilometer radius of Chacarita/Palermo, driving directly into WhatsApp.

### Phase 2: DURING (Lead) — Getting Them to Trust & Book

#### Square 4: Capture Leads
* **In plain English:** *How you get their contact info so they don't disappear forever.*
* **The Fair Tabletop Stand:** An acrylic sign placed right next to the bestseller speckled yerba mugs:  
  > **¿Querés aprender a tornear?**  
  > *Escaneá el código para recibir info de las clases y sumarte a la lista prioritaria.*  
  QR code opens a WhatsApp chat with a pre-filled prompt: *"Hola Lucía! Vi tus piezas en la feria y quiero info sobre las clases de torno."*
* **Instagram Bio Direct Link:** Replace the multi-step Linktree and static PDF with a direct `wa.me` link: *"Clases de torno en Chacarita (grupos de 6). Escribime por WhatsApp para reservar tu lugar."*
* **WhatsApp Business Tagging:** Organize inbound leads using color-coded tags: `[Interés Sábado]`, `[Interés Miércoles]`, `[Feria Lead]`, `[Taster Pendiente]`. Never lose an inquiry in chat history again.

#### Square 5: Nurture Leads
* **In plain English:** *How you stay in touch and build trust before asking them to pay.*
* **The 48-Hour Warm Video Follow-Up:** When an interested lead messages on WhatsApp but doesn't book immediately, send a warm check-in 48 hours later with a 10-second studio video:  
  *"Hola [Nombre]! Te comparto un videito del taller de ayer. Para este mes nos quedan solo 2 cupos para el torno. Si querés pasar a conocer o tenés alguna duda sobre el horario, avisame!"*
* **Instagram Stories Rhythm (3× a week, 5 mins each):** Share raw, cozy moments: opening the kiln after a glaze fire, student smiles holding their first wobbly bowl, and a weekly count of remaining seats ("Quedan 2 tornos para el grupo de los sábados").
* **Monthly Broadcast to Inactive Inquiries:** On the 20th of each month, send a gentle WhatsApp broadcast to past leads: *"Abrimos inscripciones para el próximo mes. Los cupos son 6 por grupo."*

#### Square 6: Sales Conversion
* **In plain English:** *How an interested lead turns into a paid student with zero hesitation.*
* **The Low-Risk Two-Step Ladder:**
  1. **Direct Path:** Ready students book the 4-week monthly class directly (ARS 120,000/month).
  2. **The "Probá el Torno" Taster Bridge:** Hesitant beginners who fear they have "no hand for art" or cannot commit to 4 weeks book the Saturday afternoon taster workshop (ARS 40,000, 2.5 hours).
* **The Irresistible Conversion Bridge (50% Roll-Over Credit):**  
  *"Si hacés el taller de prueba y te sumás al curso mensual dentro de las 48 horas, te bonificamos el 50% del taller ($20.000 ARS) en tu primera cuota."*  
  This eliminates the drop-off between taster and monthly class and converts "just curious" participants into committed students.
* **Frictionless Booking & Payment:** Provide immediate confirmation via WhatsApp with Mercado Pago link, CBU/Alias, studio location map, and calendar invite.

### Phase 3: AFTER (Customer) — Turning Students into Advocates

#### Square 7: World-Class Experience
* **In plain English:** *How you deliver such a memorable experience that students never want to leave.*
* **Personalized Welcome (Session 1):** When a student arrives, their wheel has a small wooden board with their name written in chalk, an apron ready, and herbal tea served in a Taller Lento yerba-green mug. Lucía guarantees every student centers and pulls their first cylinder in class 1.
* **The Same-Night Keepsake Photo:** During class, Lucía snaps 2 candid, aesthetic photos or a 5-second video of each student with clay on their hands. She sends it to their WhatsApp that evening: *"¡Qué lindo verte hoy en el torno! Mirá lo que hiciste."* (Students almost universally post this to their personal Instagram Stories, generating free word-of-mouth).
* **The Unboxing Graduation (Session 4):** Fired and glazed pieces are presented wrapped in natural kraft paper, tied with jute twine, and accompanied by a handwritten card from Lucía: *"Tu primera pieza de torno. Que la disfrutes en cada desayuno."*

#### Square 8: Lifetime Value (Retention & Expansion)
* **In plain English:** *How you keep students coming back month after month.*
* **Solve the 1-Month Drop-off with a 3-Stage Curriculum:** Students often quit after month 1 because they feel "I learned how to make a bowl, now I'm done." Replace that with an explicit progressive journey:
  * **Mes 1 (Fundamentos):** Centrado, cilindros, cuencos y desbastado.
  * **Mes 2 (Construcción & Uso):** Asas para tazas, jarras, técnicas de esmaltado combinadas.
  * **Mes 3+ (Proyecto Personal & Taller Libre):** Tetera, piezas de mayor peso, diseño de texturas.
* **The Wednesday Evening Cohort (19:00–22:00):** Captures students who drop out due to weekend getaways or social plans. Provides an after-work decompression option and adds 4–6 seats (ARS 480,000–720,000/mo).
* **Priority Re-enrollment System:** In week 3, current students receive first right of refusal for their wheel for the upcoming month before vacant spots are offered to the waitlist: *"Tenés tu torno reservado hasta este jueves para el mes que viene."*
* **Student Studio Perk:** Active students receive a 15% discount on all finished mugs, bowls, and tools.

#### Square 9: Referrals
* **In plain English:** *How you systematically get customers to bring other people.*
* **"Vení con un Amigx" Incentive:** If an existing student brings a friend to sign up for the monthly course, both receive **10% off their monthly fee**, or the referrer receives a complimentary Taller Lento handmade mug.
* **Frictionless Social Sharing:** When sending the session photos/clips to students on WhatsApp, include a friendly note: *"Si lo subís a tus historias, etiquetanos @taller.lento así lo comparto!"*
* **Palermo Fair Gift Vouchers:** Display physical gift postcards at the fair: *"Regalá una experiencia de torno — Taller Lento Chacarita"*. Fair visitors and tourists love gifting experiences rather than just physical objects.

## How to Import `plan.csv` into Google Sheets

To get the formatted 3×3 grid inside Google Sheets in under 60 seconds:

1. Open [Google Sheets](https://sheets.new) and create a blank spreadsheet.
2. Title it: **1-Page Marketing Plan — Taller Lento**.
3. Go to **File → Import → Upload**, select [plan.csv](file:///private/tmp/mp-4OloRT/plan.csv), and choose **"Replace current sheet"**.
4. Apply the recommended styling:
   * **Column Widths:** Select Columns **A, B, and C** and resize them to equal wide columns (**~390 pixels each**).
   * **Text Formatting:** Select the whole sheet → Set **Text Wrapping** to **Wrap**, and **Vertical Alignment** to **Top**.
   * **Row Heights:** Set the three square content rows (**Row 6, Row 8, and Row 10**) to a fixed height of **~180–220 pixels**.
   * **Phase Bands:**
     * **Row 5 (BEFORE):** Fill background with soft sage green (`#D9EAD3`), bold text.
     * **Row 7 (DURING):** Fill background with soft ochre / amber (`#FCE5CD`), bold text.
     * **Row 9 (AFTER):** Fill background with soft terracotta / rust (`#F4CCCC`), bold text.
   * **Header Freezing:** Select Row 4 → **View → Freeze → Up to row 4**.
