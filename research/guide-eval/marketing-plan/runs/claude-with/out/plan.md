# 1-Page Marketing Plan — Taller Lento

*Lucía Ferreyra · ceramics studio · Chacarita, Buenos Aires · drafted 3 October 2026*

> This draft comes from your brief. You weren't available, so where something was missing I made a best guess. Every guess is listed under **"Assumptions I made"** at the bottom. Change anything that doesn't feel right. This is your plan.

## The foundation

**The item:** Wheel-throwing classes at Taller Lento. That means the monthly group (Saturdays 10–13, max 6 people, ARS 120,000/month, clay and firing included) and the 2.5-hour taster "Probá el torno" (ARS 40,000). The mugs matter too, but mostly as the shop window that brings people into the studio.

**The sale (what "it worked" means):** A stranger books a class (the monthly group, or a taster that turns into the monthly) and then **stays past their second month**. A tourist buying one mug is nice, but it doesn't count as a sale in this plan.

**6-month target (by early April 2027):** Saturday group full (6/6) every month · a Wednesday 19–22 group running with 4+ students · more students staying past month 2.

**Limits:** about 5 hours a week of marketing · about US$50 a month (ARS 70,000), not counting the fair stall.

## BEFORE: strangers who don't know you yet

### 1. Target market
*In plain words: who exactly this is for. Not "everyone".*

- **Main:** women aged 28–45 with office, design or tech jobs who live in Chacarita, Colegiales, Villa Crespo or Palermo. They're tired of screens and want a calm weekly ritual with their hands. They often come with a friend.
- **The Wednesday group** is for the same women who can't give up their Saturday morning. For them it's an after-work plan.
- **Second group:** English-speaking expats and digital nomads in the same barrios. You can teach the class in English.
- **Not the target for classes:** tourists (they buy one mug and leave within a week) and cafés asking for wholesale. Say no kindly: the margins are bad and it eats kiln space.

### 2. Message
*In plain words: what you say to those people so they care.*

- **Main line:** ES *"3 horas por semana lejos de la pantalla, con las manos en el barro."* / EN *"Three hours a week off-screen, hands in clay."*
- **Proof that makes it real:** max 6 people · clay and firing included · you keep everything you make · 10 minutes' walk from Lacroze.
- **At the fair, the hook is the mug in their hand:** *"¿Te gusta? Te enseño a hacerla."* / *"Like it? I'll teach you to make it."*
- **The first step is small and low-risk:** the taster "Probá el torno".
- Write every post and caption in **Spanish and English**.

### 3. Media
*In plain words: where you'll reach those people. Go where they already are, not just where it's easy to post.*

- **The Sunday fair** is your best room: people are already touching your work and asking "do you give classes?"
- **Instagram:** 2 Reels of the wheel a week (they get 5–10x the views of photos), with bilingual captions and local hashtags (#Chacarita #ceramica #clasesdeceramica).
- **Google Maps:** a free listing, "Taller Lento – clases de cerámica". People search "clases de cerámica cerca de mí", and right now you don't show up.
- **Expat groups:** one post a month in Buenos Aires expat groups (Facebook or WhatsApp) about the class in English.
- **About US$25/month:** boost your best Reel to women aged 25–45 in your 4 barrios.

## DURING: people who showed interest

### 4. Capture leads
*In plain words: a "lead" is someone who showed interest **and gave you a way to contact them** (here, their WhatsApp). Today "follow me on Instagram" is where it ends. This square fixes that.*

- **At the fair:** a small sign and cards with a **QR code that opens WhatsApp with a ready-written message** ("Hola! Quiero info de clases"). Make it free with a `wa.me` link and any free QR generator. Also keep a notebook on the table: *"Avisame del próximo taller de prueba"* (name + WhatsApp).
- **Instagram bio:** replace the PDF price list with the same WhatsApp link plus "Próximo taller de prueba / Next taster: [date]".
- **Switch to WhatsApp Business** (free, same number). Use labels to sort people: *Interesada · Taster · Alumna · Ex-alumna*.
- **One Google Sheet called "Leads"** with columns: name · WhatsApp · where they came from (fair / IG / Maps / friend) · date · status.

### 5. Nurture leads
*In plain words: stay in touch, gently, so they trust you before you ask them to book.*

- **Within 48 hours of each fair,** message everyone from the notebook and QR with a short Reel and the next taster date.
- **Every 2 weeks, one WhatsApp broadcast:** a kiln photo, seats left, the next taster. (Broadcasts only reach people who saved your number, so ask them to save it.) Don't send more often than that.
- **Instagram stories:** students' pieces (ask permission first), kiln openings, "a Saturday at the taller".
- Expats get the English version of every message.

### 6. Sales conversion
*In plain words: how an interested person becomes a booked student who stays.*

- **A fixed taster date every month** (e.g. last Saturday of the month, 15:00). A date is much easier to sell than "when enough people ask".
- **Use the kiln as your follow-up.** Taster pieces are fired later, so you always have a reason to write back: *"¡Tu taza salió del horno! There's a seat on Saturday / Wednesday."* Picking up the piece is also the moment to offer the monthly class face to face.
- **Taster-to-monthly credit:** join the monthly within 7 days and get ARS 20,000 off the first month.
- **Friends together:** 2 friends who sign up together get 10% off each.
- **Wednesday group:** start collecting a "Wednesday list" now. The group opens as soon as **4 people pay the first month** (Mercado Pago link).

## AFTER: your students

### 7. World-class experience
*In plain words: deliver so well that students become fans.*

- **A welcome WhatsApp the day before the first class:** address, how to walk from Lacroze, what to wear, aprons provided. Save it as a quick reply in WhatsApp Business so it's one tap.
- **Everyone leaves the first class having thrown something,** and you send each person a photo of their first piece.
- **Give each month a goal:** week 1 centering → week 2 cylinders → week 3 mugs and bowls → week 4 trimming and glazing (yes, they can use the yerba green).
- **Small rituals:** a mate break every class, and kiln opening as a small celebration.
- **Missed a class?** Message the same day: *"We missed you, here's what we did."* People who drift away usually start by missing one class.

### 8. Lifetime value
*In plain words: keep each student longer and let them spend more if they want to. Your brief says the 3+ month students are your best people, so this is the most important square.*

- **Make-up classes:** miss a Saturday, come on Wednesday (and the other way round). This answers "my schedule changed", the #1 reason students leave.
- **Renewal message 1 week before the month ends:** *"Your seat is saved until [date]"* plus what they'll make next month.
- **Projects for months 2–3:** a set of 4 matching mugs, a serving bowl, their own glaze test. Give people a reason to stay.
- **3-month pack:** ARS 330,000 paid up front (instead of 360,000).
- **Students get 15% off mugs.** They already buy them as gifts.
- **Taster gift vouchers:** sell them at the fair to local gift buyers. **Día de la Madre is Sunday 18 October**, and Christmas comes after that.
- **Every ~3 months, message ex-students:** *"A seat opened on Wednesday."*

### 9. Referrals
*In plain words: deliberately get happy students to bring others. Most of your students already came this way, so make it a habit instead of luck.*

- **"Traé una amiga":** when a friend you bring signs up for a month, you get a mug of your choice. It costs you clay and kiln space, but it's worth ARS 28,000 to them.
- **Ask at happy moments:** kiln opening, end of month 3.
- **Make it easy:** a ready-to-forward WhatsApp message with the QR and the next taster date.
- **Ask happy students for a Google Maps review,** and to tag @taller.lento when they post their pieces.

## Things deliberately left out

Wholesale to cafés, international shipping, a website and an email list. They would cost time you don't have and wouldn't move the 6-month goal. WhatsApp + Instagram + Google Maps + one Sheet is enough.

## Assumptions I made (please check)

1. **The sale = a booked class that sticks,** not mug sales. Taken from your "when I picture it worked…" paragraph.
2. **The prices for the new offers are my suggestions:** ARS 20,000 taster credit, 10% off for friends signing up together, ARS 330,000 3-month pack, 15% off mugs for students, a free mug per referral. Adjust them to your margins.
3. **The Wednesday group** opens once 4 people have paid, not on a fixed date. Buenos Aires empties in January, so if it hasn't filled by December, aim for a start in early March when routines restart.
4. **Make-up classes between groups** only work once the Wednesday group exists. Until then, offer a make-up seat in the monthly taster.
5. **The monthly taster** is fixed to the last Saturday afternoon of the month. Pick another slot if that one clashes.
6. **Exchange rate:** about ARS 1,400 = US$1, based on your own figures (US$50 ≈ ARS 70,000).
