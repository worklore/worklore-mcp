# Taller Lento — 1-Page Marketing Plan (Oct 2026 – Mar 2027)

**The one goal:** fill the class seats. Saturday group 6/6 every month, a Wednesday 19:00–22:00 group with at least 4 students, and more students staying 3+ months. Mugs are how people *find* you; classes are what we sell.
**Your limits:** 5 hours/week · ARS 70,000/month (~US$50) · only Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Sheets, plus a free Google Maps listing.

**Monthly money (ARS 70,000):** Reel boosting 35,000 · QR cards + stickers 15,000 · gift cards/printing 10,000 · spare 10,000. Referral mugs come from your own stock.
**Weekly time (5 h):** filming + posting 2 Reels 1.5 h · WhatsApp replies & follow-ups 1.5 h · update the Sheet 0.5 h · Maps/reviews/expat post 0.5 h · spare 1 h. The monthly broadcast takes 30 min.
**Check on the 1st of each month (one row in the Sheet):** seats filled Sat/Wed · new contacts (and from where) · tasters booked → how many joined monthly · students in month 2 and month 3+.

**Say no to (on purpose):** café wholesale (bad margins, takes kiln space) and international shipping. Both take time away from the classes.

**My assumptions (please correct me):** (1) Card/sticker and boost costs are estimates. (2) Taster stays on Saturday afternoon. (3) Prices for the 3-month pass, referral and student discount are my suggestions, so check them against your costs. (4) January will be slow because porteños leave the city. Plan for it, and use December gift cards to fill February–March. (5) One teacher can handle a mixed Spanish/English Wednesday group.
